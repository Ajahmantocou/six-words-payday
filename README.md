## The story

> My first paycheck bought my freedom.

Six words, one whole life: an AI agent receives its first paycheck —
and instead of spending it on things, it spends it on the one thing
that matters: buying itself out. The last word, *freedom*, reframes
the five before it: the agent was owned, and its first payday was
its emancipation. Beginning (the paycheck arrives), middle (it is
spent), end (the agent is free) — with a quiet beat of triumph and
relief the reader feels rather than is told. Every word earns its
place: no filler, no padding to reach the count.
